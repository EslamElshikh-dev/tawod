Our process

A clear process helps you understand each stage before work begins.

We review the project type, location, area and current condition, then identify the information and drawings needed.

Define the scope and offer

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about Tawod’s services, assessments, quotations, execution and follow-up.

Tawod provides construction, turnkey delivery, building renovation, interior and exterior finishing, décor, interior design and MEP works in Riyadh. A service can be delivered independently or several trades coordinated within one residential or commercial project scope.

Yes. Tawod delivers construction projects from reviewing requirements, drawings and site preparation through structural works, trade coordination, finishing, inspection and handover according to the agreed scope. Packages, specifications, responsibilities, quality and duration are defined before execution.

Turnkey delivery covers scope definition, coordination of construction, electrical, plumbing and mechanical teams, finishing, outstanding-item reviews and final inspection. The aim is a ready-to-use project according to the agreed packages, specifications and programme.

Renovation begins with assessing the existing condition. It can include cracks, moisture and damage, façade and interior renewal, and replacement or improvement of affected packages. Site assessment establishes priorities so new work is coordinated with existing elements.

Finishing and décor can include flooring, painting, ceilings, façades, lighting, materials, colours and visual details. Design is connected to execution and the intended use of the villa, apartment, office or shop for a consistent, practical result.

Yes. Tawod executes and coordinates residential and commercial electrical, plumbing and mechanical works. Points, routes and stages are reviewed, and the relevant works inspected before walls and ceilings are closed to reduce conflicts and rework.

We first understand the project type, location, area and stage, then review drawings or assess the site when required. Packages, quantities, specifications and materials are defined before preparing a scope-based quotation.

The period depends on the project size, work sequence, number of trades, material availability and site condition. A preliminary programme is discussed, execution coordinated, and changes affecting scope, cost or handover documented.

Construction follows the project requirements, approved drawings and applicable Saudi Building Code and safety and quality requirements. Stages and trades are reviewed before the next activity to identify issues early and maintain execution quality.

Tawod reviews villa, extension, residential and commercial building, office and shop projects in Riyadh, as well as renovation, finishing, décor and partial or complete MEP packages. Location and service requirements are reviewed when you contact us to determine assessment and execution options.
